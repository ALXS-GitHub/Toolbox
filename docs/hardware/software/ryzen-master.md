# AMD Ryzen Master

Ryzen Master shows the frequency, temperature and power draw of every core of the [Ryzen 7 9800X3D](/hardware/computer)
in real time, and lets you tune its limits (Precision Boost Overdrive, Curve Optimizer). I open it now and then to check
temperatures after a change or under heavy load.
