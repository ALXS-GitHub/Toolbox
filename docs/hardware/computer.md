# My PC

My main computer is a desktop PC I built in the summer of 2026 to replace my
[old laptop](/archive/hardware/asus-tuf-f17). It does everything: development during the day — compiling Rust, running
several dev servers and agents in parallel — and gaming at night, mostly competitive games at 1440p on a 240 Hz screen.

## The build

| Part | Model |
|---|---|
| **CPU** | AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D (8 cores, 16 threads) |
| **GPU** | Gigabyte Radeon RX 9070 XT Gaming OC ICE, 16 GB |
| **Motherboard** | Gigabyte B850 AORUS ELITE WIFI7 ICE |
| **Memory** | 32 GB DDR5-6000 CL30 (2 × 16 GB Kingston Fury Beast, EXPO profile) |
| **Storage** | Samsung 990 EVO Plus, 2 TB NVMe |
| **Cooling** | Thermalright Peerless Assassin 120 SE ARGB |
| **Case** | Lian Li LANCOOL 217 INF |
| **Power supply** | Corsair RM850e, 850 W |

No sound card, network card or optical drive: the motherboard provides audio, 2.5 Gb Ethernet, Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth.
The whole build is white, from the parts to the case.

## The choices

- **The 9800X3D** and its large stacked L3 cache (*3D V-Cache*): the fastest gaming CPU, and its 8 cores are plenty for
  my builds. At 1440p, the graphics card is the limit anyway.
- **The RX 9070 XT**: the best performance per euro for high-refresh 1440p gaming.
- **32 GB of DDR5-6000 CL30**: the sweet spot for this CPU, with low latency.
- **An airflow-first case** and a dual-tower air cooler: the 9800X3D stays cool and quiet without liquid cooling.
- **A B850 board** rather than an X870: everything I need (Wi-Fi 7, three M.2 slots, front USB-C), without paying for
  overclocking.
