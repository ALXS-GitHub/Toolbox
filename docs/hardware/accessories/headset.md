# ASUS ROG Delta II

My headset for gaming, calls and music. Its key feature is **DualFlow**: it receives PC audio over 2.4 GHz (no latency)
and phone audio over Bluetooth at the same time, and mixes both. I can stay in a game and take a call without touching
anything.

Battery life exceeds **110 hours** on 2.4 GHz: I charge it about once a month, and a quarter of an hour of charging gives
around ten hours. The detachable mic is enough for Discord and calls, without a desk microphone.

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Connection** | 2.4 GHz, Bluetooth and 3.5 mm jack, 2.4 GHz + Bluetooth at once |
| **Drivers** | 50 mm, 24-bit / 96 kHz audio |
| **Battery** | 110+ h (15 min charge = 11 h) |
| **Weight** | 318 g |
| **Mic** | detachable, super-wideband |
