# DeepSeek

DeepSeek is an assistant whose models, released as open source, surprised everyone with their reasoning and coding
level for a very low training cost.

## Why I stopped

I tried it when it came out, out of curiosity, without making it an everyday tool:
[ChatGPT](/tools/ai/assistants/chatgpt) and [Claude](/tools/ai/assistants/claude) remain my assistants.
