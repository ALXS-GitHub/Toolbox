# Mistral

Le Chat is the assistant from Mistral AI, a French company that also releases some of its models as open source. It
is fast and very good in French.

## Why I stopped

I tried it as a French alternative, but [ChatGPT](/tools/ai/assistants/chatgpt) and
[Claude](/tools/ai/assistants/claude) cover my daily needs.
