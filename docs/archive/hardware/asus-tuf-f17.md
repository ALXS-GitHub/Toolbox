# ASUS TUF Gaming F17

For several years this 17.3-inch laptop was my only computer: development by day, gaming at night.

| Part | Model |
|---|---|
| **CPU** | Intel Core i7-11800H (8 cores, 16 threads) |
| **GPU** | NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop, 6 GB |
| **Memory** | 16 GB DDR4-3200 |
| **Storage** | 1 TB NVMe PCIe 3.0 |
| **Screen** | 17.3" Full HD IPS, 144 Hz |

## Why I replaced it

It was reaching its limits, both in games and when running several projects and agents in parallel. I replaced it with
a much more powerful [desktop PC](/hardware/computer), with two 1440p [screens](/hardware/monitors).
