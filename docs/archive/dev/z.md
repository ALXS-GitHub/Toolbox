# z

z, ported as a PowerShell module, remembers visited folders and takes you back by typing part of their name.

## Why I stopped

[zoxide](/tools/dev/cli/zoxide) does the same, better: faster, available in every shell, with an interactive picker (`zi`). [CortX](/projects/cortx) initialises it.
