# BakkesMod

BakkesMod was the training companion of almost every [Rocket League](/games/rocket-league) player on PC: training tools,
statistics, automatic replay saving and fan-made items.

## Why I stopped

Since Easy Anti-Cheat arrived in Rocket League in April 2026, mods no longer work online, and BakkesMod ended its
development. The game took over some of its features.
