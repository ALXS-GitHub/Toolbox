# NotebookLM

NotebookLM is a Google tool, built on [Gemini](/tools/ai/assistants/gemini), that only works on the sources you give
it: PDFs, documents, web pages, YouTube videos. Its answers cite the passage they come from, which makes them easy to
check, and it makes things up far less than a general assistant.

It is what I use to understand a large document or a set of sources: ask questions about it, get a summary, or listen
to the "podcast" it generates, an audio conversation between two voices presenting the topic.
