# ChatGPT

ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational assistant, and one of the two I use every day, with
[Claude](/tools/ai/assistants/claude). I use it on the web, in the desktop app and on my phone.

## How I use it

The two assistants overlap a lot, and I switch from one to the other depending on the task: a second opinion on a
question, a web search, an image to generate, a voice conversation. Having two different models at hand is useful in
itself: when one gets stuck, the other often sees the problem differently.

The ChatGPT subscription also gives access to [Codex](/tools/ai/coding/codex), OpenAI's coding agent, which I use on the
command line next to Claude Code.

## What it replaced

Over time, ChatGPT and Claude took the place of the other assistants I had tried: [Perplexity](/archive/ai/perplexity),
[Grok](/archive/ai/grok) and [DeepSeek](/archive/ai/deepseek), now in the archive.
