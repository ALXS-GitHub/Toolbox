# Mistral

Le Chat is the assistant from Mistral AI, a French company that also releases some of its models as open source. It
is fast and very good in French.

I am not using it at the moment: [ChatGPT](/tools/ai/assistants/chatgpt) and [Claude](/tools/ai/assistants/claude)
cover my daily needs. I keep it in mind as an alternative, especially for its open models, which you can run yourself.
