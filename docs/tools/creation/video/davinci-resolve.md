# DaVinci Resolve

DaVinci Resolve is professional video editing software — editing, colour grading, effects, audio mixing — whose free
version is more than enough for personal use. I open it rarely, when a video needs real editing: several tracks,
transitions, text. For a simple cut or conversion, [FFmpeg](/tools/creation/video/ffmpeg) is enough.
