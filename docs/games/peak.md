# PEAK

PEAK is a co-op climbing game by Aggro Crab and Landfall. Up to four scouts stranded on an island must climb to the top
of a mountain to call for rescue. You help each other at every step: lending a hand, placing ropes and pitons for those
who follow, sharing questionable food, and managing a stamina that shrinks with every injury.

The mountain changes every 24 hours, and every run ends in laughter. It is one of my current games with friends, on
[Discord](/tools/web/communication/discord).
