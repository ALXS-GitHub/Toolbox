# Genshin Impact

Genshin Impact is a free open-world action RPG by HoYoverse. Its world, Teyvat, is huge and beautiful, and its combat
relies on seven elements whose combinations trigger reactions: building a team means looking for the best pairings. I
play it now and then, mostly to explore new regions.
