# Rocket League

Rocket League is football with rocket-powered cars: you jump, fly and hit a giant ball towards the opponent's goal. The
rules fit in one sentence, but mastery takes hundreds of hours — aerial control, dribbles, rotations with teammates. It
is my current competitive game, mostly ranked 2v2 and 3v3.

## My setup

I play on my [240 Hz screen](/hardware/monitors), where smoothness really helps reading the ball, with my
[magnetic-switch keyboard](/hardware/accessories/keyboard) and its Rapid Trigger. I even wrote my own mod to customise
how cars look: [ALXS-RL-Mod](/projects/alxs-rl-mod).

## Since the anti-cheat

Since April 2026, Rocket League uses **Easy Anti-Cheat** on PC. Playing online requires turning it on, and mods no longer
run: [BakkesMod](/archive/games/bakkesmod), which I used for training, is therefore in the archive. The game took over
some of its features, such as showing ranked level and randomised training.
