# No Man's Sky

No Man's Sky is a space exploration game in a procedurally generated universe: billions of planets, each with its own
terrain, weather, fauna and flora. You take off from a planet, cross space without a loading screen and land on the
next one.

The game came a long way: disappointing at launch in 2016, it was transformed by years of free updates (multiplayer,
bases, freighters, expeditions). I come back to it now and then, for an expedition or just to explore.
