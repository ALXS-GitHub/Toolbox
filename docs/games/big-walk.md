# Big Walk

Big Walk is the co-op game from House House, the studio behind Untitled Goose Game, released in August 2026. You go
hiking with friends through a large open world full of puzzles and discoveries, and almost everything relies on
communication: finding your way, giving directions, solving together what nobody can do alone. You can play with two
or up to twelve.

It is the kind of game where you spend as much time chatting as moving forward, and that is the whole point.
