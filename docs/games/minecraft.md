# Minecraft

Minecraft needs no introduction: an endlessly generated world of blocks, where you explore, survive and build whatever
you want. I come back to it now and then, most often with friends and **modpacks** — sets of mods that transform the
game (technology, magic, adventure) — installed in one click with [CurseForge](/games/mods/curseforge).
